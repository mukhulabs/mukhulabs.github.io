Willingly: AI Notes & Notebook – Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy explains how Willingly: AI Notes & Notebook ("the app"), published by Mukhu Labs, handles your information. Last updated: 21 November 2026. 1. Your notes stay on your phone Notes, notebooks, drawings, PDFs, pictures, voice recordings, folders and tags are stored only on your device. You can use the app fully without creating an account. 2. Optional account and cloud sync If you choose to sign in, we use Google Firebase Authentication (your email address) and Cloud Firestore to back up your notes and sync them between your devices. Pictures, PDF pages and audio files are not uploaded. You can sign out or delete your cloud account at any time in Account & sync. 3. Shared notes When you share a note, its title, text and comments are stored in Firestore so that people who have the share code can view and edit it. Anyone you give the code to can see that note. Stop sharing at any time from the note. 4. AI features AI features (summary, key points, flashcards, translation, reading handwriting, transcription, Ask your notes) work only when you tap them. The text, page image or recording you choose is sent to Google's Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic to create the result. It is not used by Mukhu Labs for any other purpose. Do not use AI features on information you do not want to send. 5. On-device features Text scanning (OCR) and handwriting-to-text run on your phone with Google ML Kit; a language model may be downloaded once. Voice typing and voice commands use your phone's speech recogniser, which may process audio according to your device settings. 6. Ads The free version shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings. 7. Purchases Purchases (Lifetime Pro, AI credit packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. 8. Permissions Microphone: voice notes, voice typing and voice commands. Camera (through your camera app): taking photos for notes. Notifications: reminders. Biometrics: unlocking the private vault. Each is used only for the feature you start. 9. Children The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. 10. Security and deletion Vault PINs are stored only as a secure hash on your device. Deleting a note in the app removes it from your device and, if you use sync, from the cloud. Uninstalling the app removes local data. To delete cloud data, use Delete account in the app or write to us. 11. Changes and contact We may update this policy; the date above will change.
